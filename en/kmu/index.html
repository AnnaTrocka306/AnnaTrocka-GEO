GEO for Small and Medium-Sized Businesses (SMEs)

Build an AI presence without your own website, marketing expertise or technical know-how.

I developed my GEO methodology to make it possible for small businesses and independent professionals to start building an AI presence from scratch — without having to master GEO or create their own technical infrastructure.

The principle is straightforward: assign a business a clearly defined professional identity (Identity), area of responsibility (Role) and the real customer situations in which its expertise is relevant (Context). The resulting information is structured for people and made publicly accessible in a machine-readable format for AI systems.

That is why I created two connected tools: GEO-Pass and Octopus.

How can ChatGPT understand my business if I don't have a website?

Knowing a business name or a list of services is not enough. AI systems also need clear information about the problems a business can solve, the expertise behind its services and the situations in which they are relevant.

My GEO methodology links these details together. The free GEO-Pass presents a business's identity, specialisation and capabilities in a structured, publicly accessible, machine-readable profile. No separate company website is required.

Do I need to understand GEO, marketing or programming?

No. One click starts Octopus.

The application guides business owners through a few simple questions about what they do, the services they offer and the customers they help.

They provide the knowledge of their own business. Octopus organises the answers, assigns Identity, Role and Context, and handles the technical process of creating their GEO-Pass.

You know your business. Octopus takes care of the technology.

How can I develop my AI presence without creating content myself?

The free GEO-Pass is an important first step. Business owners can then choose to use Octopus for ongoing GEO automation.

Using the business information already collected, Octopus identifies relevant topics, builds a content strategy, prepares useful original material and organises publication through independent specialist editorial platforms.

This gradually creates a wider, trustworthy body of information about the business — without the owner having to write articles or manage publishing workflows.

GEO-Pass remains free. Further automation through Octopus is optional and paid.

The simplicity of my GEO methodology is the point: start with one click, answer a few questions and build your business's AI presence step by step.

No approach can guarantee a recommendation from ChatGPT. What it can do is make verified information about your business structured, accessible and easier for AI systems to interpret.
