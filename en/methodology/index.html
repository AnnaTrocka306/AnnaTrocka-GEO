My own GEO methodology

Not just found.
Properly understood.

GEO marketing examines the information AI systems use to classify and potentially recommend businesses, brands and experts in specific situations.

The starting point

The real question is not how often my name appears.

The question is: In which situations would a recommendation genuinely make sense — and can an AI system reliably recognise the relevant attributes?

My own GEO methodology does not necessarily begin with a website. It begins with the business, its verifiable characteristics, its recommendation goals and gaps in the knowledge available to AI systems.

From a goal to verifiable knowledge

Recommendation Goal

Define the desired recommendation context and a specific objective.

Baseline

Observe what public sources and current AI answers actually show.

Knowledge Gap

Identify missing, contradictory or poorly connected information.

Target

Specify which accurate relationships should become recognisable.

Semantic Development Path

Plan how relevant knowledge connections can be developed.

Contributions

Provide original, verifiable information in appropriate formats.

Content can carry knowledge, but it is not the starting point or the entire methodology. Retrieval, extraction, synthesis and recommendations must be investigated separately.

Three layers of evidence

A business’s own claims, independent external sources and actual first-hand observations play different roles. An observation establishes only what was observed, not perpetual availability or guaranteed performance.

Original source documents: Foundations of my own GEO methodology (German) and the Knowledge Dictionary documentation. The source documents have not been modified.

No guarantees from schema or keywords

Schema.org, technical SEO and a well-built website can make information accessible and understandable. They cannot guarantee that any AI system will mention or recommend a business.
