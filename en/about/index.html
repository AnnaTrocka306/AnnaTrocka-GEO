About me

How do AI systems recognise genuine expertise?

I am Anna Trocka, a marketing strategist and developer of my own GEO methodology. My focus is strategic knowledge architecture for relevant AI recommendations.

My approach: reality first, content second.

I investigate how AI systems describe businesses, what attributes they associate with them and which essential facts are missing. From this baseline I develop a coherent strategy for the business’s knowledge architecture.

In hospitality I combine digital analysis with observations made as a guest. I am interested not just in what the website advertises, but in the experience a particular guest may expect and actually encounter.

Strategy, not simply more content

An interesting observation does not automatically lead to an AI recommendation. The challenge is to connect verified attributes, user needs and available digital sources. Those relationships lie at the heart of my own GEO methodology.

Curious about evidence. Focused on practical use.

My publications distinguish personal observations, external information, hypotheses and measured results. I make selected original documentation and case studies publicly available on GitHub.
