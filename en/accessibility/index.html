Accessibility

A website should be understandable and usable by everyone.

This preview is developed with WCAG 2.2 Level AA in mind. A completed conformity audit or final legal assessment under Germany’s BFSG has not yet been carried out.

Included in this preview

  • Structured headings and semantic HTML landmarks.
  • A skip link and keyboard-accessible navigation.
  • Visible focus indicators for interactive elements.
  • Responsive layout, zoom-friendly text and reduced-motion support.
  • Alternative descriptions for informative images.
  • No embedded tracking or video widgets in the first prototype.

What still needs testing

Colour contrast, keyboard navigation and mobile rendering on multiple devices, screen readers, HTML and WCAG testing, any future contact forms, technical documentation and a possible legally required accessibility declaration.

Germany’s Accessibility Strengthening Act (BFSG) does not automatically apply to every informational business website. The actual services offered and potential exceptions need a separate assessment.
