# On-Site Verification

## Definition

**On-Site Verification** describes the structured verification of whether digitally identifiable information, attributes and expectations can be confirmed in the real service, product or actual usage situation of an entity.

It connects the [AI and Digital Perception](https://github.com/AnnaTrocka306/AnnaTrocka-GEO/blob/main/docs/knowledge-base/Knowledge_Dictionary/AI_and_Digital_Perception_EN.md) of an entity with its observable reality and thereby enables a comparison between digitally communicated attributes and characteristics that are actually present or experienced.

On-Site Verification can relate to a [Business Entity](https://github.com/AnnaTrocka306/AnnaTrocka-GEO/blob/main/docs/knowledge-base/Knowledge_Dictionary/Business_Entity.md), a [Service Entity](https://github.com/AnnaTrocka306/AnnaTrocka-GEO/blob/main/docs/knowledge-base/Knowledge_Dictionary/Service_Entity.md), or a [Product Entity](https://github.com/AnnaTrocka306/AnnaTrocka-GEO/blob/main/docs/knowledge-base/Knowledge_Dictionary/Product_Entity.md). It examines whether attributes and decision-relevant factors that are important within a [Recommendation Situation](https://github.com/AnnaTrocka306/AnnaTrocka-GEO/blob/main/docs/knowledge-base/Knowledge_Dictionary/Recommendation_Situation_EN.md) are actually present and verifiable in the real usage situation.

On-Site Verification is not intended as a general quality assessment of an entity and should not be equated with a traditional inspection or standardized mystery check. Its function is to **systematically verify alignment or deviation between digitally communicated expectations and observable reality**.
