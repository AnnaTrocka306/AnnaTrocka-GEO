# AI and Digital Perception

## Definition

**AI and Digital Perception** describes the overall image of an entity that emerges from digitally available information and information relationships and through which the entity can be recognized, classified and associated by AI systems with specific attributes, roles, contexts, target groups and potential recommendation situations.

It emerges from the interaction of different digitally accessible information sources and signals. These may include information published by the entity itself, external sources, editorial publications, platform information, reviews and other publicly accessible information relationships.

AI and Digital Perception is not equivalent to the positioning defined by the entity itself. Differences may exist between the intended positioning of an entity and its actually observable digital perception.

The term does not imply access to internal models, algorithms, weightings or non-public decision-making processes of an AI system. It refers exclusively to the **externally observable interpretation and classification of an entity** that can be derived from available digital information as well as from observable AI responses, associations and recommendations.
