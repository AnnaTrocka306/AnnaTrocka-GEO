# GEO Recommendation Gap

## Definition

A **GEO Recommendation Gap** describes a relevant discrepancy within the digital information and recommendation structure of an entity that can prevent AI systems from identifying, classifying, or consistently understanding its suitability for specific [Recommendation Situations](https://github.com/AnnaTrocka306/AnnaTrocka-GEO/blob/main/docs/knowledge-base/Knowledge_Dictionary/Recommendation_Situation_EN.md).

Such a gap can arise when attributes that actually exist are not sufficiently connected to the entity digitally, when relevant information is missing or too weakly represented, when different sources communicate conflicting signals, or when individual attributes within the [AI and Digital Perception](https://github.com/AnnaTrocka306/AnnaTrocka-GEO/blob/main/docs/knowledge-base/Knowledge_Dictionary/AI_and_Digital_Perception_EN.md) appear more or less prominent than their actual relevance and positioning would justify.

A GEO Recommendation Gap can also exist when an expectation created digitally is not sufficiently confirmed by the real service or product. Such a discrepancy can become visible through [On-Site Verification](https://github.com/AnnaTrocka306/AnnaTrocka-GEO/blob/main/docs/knowledge-base/Knowledge_Dictionary/On_Site_Verification_EN.md) or other appropriate forms of reality verification.

The term therefore does not automatically describe an operational defect. It describes a **recommendation-relevant discrepancy between actual reality, digital perception, information structure, and the situational recommendability of an entity**.
