SEO, social media and GEO: content with different jobs

SEO: get found

Useful pages are prepared for search engines so people can find them and visit your website.

Social media: spark interest

Videos, photos and posts attract attention, build trust and create demand.

GEO: be relevant to a need

Well-structured information helps AI understand who you help, what problems you solve and when your skills make sense.

The central GEO question: Why might you be a good fit for this particular person's problem?

Octopus is built to do the GEO work for you

Effective GEO takes marketing judgment, an understanding of customer needs, structured information and the technical requirements of different platforms. Business owners shouldn't need to master all of that.

Octopus is designed to learn about your services and priorities, then shape an individual strategy: Which areas matter most? What do potential clients ask? What evidence is missing?

Using that knowledge, the planned system will automatically draft, check and publish useful articles, interviews and posts through the channels you choose and connect.

It's not about churning out articles. It's about explaining your value in situations where clients genuinely need help.

What Octopus is being built to do

One connected workflow, from your professional identity to useful content and distribution. These capabilities are part of the product roadmap.

Expert passports with structured data

A professional profile with machine-readable Schema.org data in JSON-LD. It helps systems interpret your identity, services and related facts; it is not a direct feed into ChatGPT recommendations.

Posts built around real customer needs

LinkedIn and other social posts that explain when your expertise is relevant. They build useful recommendation context, not a magic trigger in an AI algorithm.

GEO-focused articles and interviews

Helpful expert pieces, interviews and answers grounded in your verified knowledge base and tailored to each channel.

Landing pages and complete websites

Pages with a clear message, well-organised content and practical technical foundations for both GEO and SEO.

Monitoring and analytics

Checks on how AI describes your work, where it mentions you and what needs correcting, plus available publication and bot-visit statistics.

Automatic publishing

Distribution to your authorised platforms. Independent publishers still decide what they accept and publish.

Why go beyond your own website or LinkedIn?

Your own channels present your expertise in your own words. An independent publisher can explore it differently – through an interview, practical commentary or a helpful industry roundup.

These stories give people and AI additional places to find information about you and understand your experience in context.

Octopus is intended to share material across your own channels and suitable external platforms. Independent editors decide what they publish. A positive mention cannot be bought.

Outside coverage does not automatically carry more weight with ChatGPT. Its potential value is in extra facts and perspectives.

Small steps. A clearer picture of you.

  1. Step 1. GEO Pass: the introduction. Octopus learns who you are, what you do and up to three professional areas you want to prioritise.
  2. Step 2. Explore one priority area. What client problems do you solve? How do you help? When are you useful, and what outcomes can clients reasonably expect?
  3. Step 3. One detail at a time. Your experience, distinctive qualities, other skills, and professional and commercial priorities become part of a growing knowledge base.

Why not collect everything at once? Smaller steps prevent mixed-up services and contradictions, and help preserve what matters. Other skills aren't discarded – we establish a sensible order.

That structured knowledge base is what Octopus will use to shape the strategy and generate relevant content.

Start free. Stay in charge of your budget.

The planned entry point is a free GEO Pass. You can start structuring your professional identity without first committing money to marketing.

After that, you choose what you can afford for each next step – even if your budget is small. No compulsory monthly subscription, agency retainer or need to learn GEO yourself.

Spending more can speed up the creation and distribution of planned materials. It cannot guarantee an earlier ChatGPT recommendation.
