GEO Pass

Your machine-readable professional profile.

The GEO Pass puts your qualifications, areas of expertise, services and the problems you help solve into a structured, machine-readable professional profile. It is designed to help AI systems better interpret your work, even without your own website.

It's a starting point, not a promise of recommendations from ChatGPT or other AI systems.
