Why does this matter for your business?

ChatGPT and other AI systems draw on publicly accessible information online when answering questions about businesses and professionals.

When relevant information about your services appears across different trustworthy websites, AI systems have more context to understand what you offer and the situations in which you can help.

This is where GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) comes in.

An article in an independent regional online magazine adds another public source of information about your business. It can help search engines find your services and increase the opportunities for your expertise to be considered in relevant AI-powered recommendations.

What is GEO? Learn more ↗

How does it work?

Octopus works with selected independent editorial partners, including non-commercial regional online magazines. Each publication decides for itself which experts and businesses it wishes to introduce to its readers.

  1. A free editorial enquiry

    Octopus contacts a suitable editorial partner and suggests featuring your business or professional expertise.

    For example: “We've come across an interesting expert in your region. Would your readers be interested in learning about them?”

    Sending this enquiry costs you nothing.

  2. The editor makes the decision

    Our partners don't automatically accept every proposed article. They decide whether the topic fits their publication.

    99% positive responses: In 99% of our previous enquiries, we've received approval to publish an article.

  3. Octopus writes your article

    After the positive response, Octopus automatically prepares a professional article of up to 500 words about your business, services and strengths.

    You review the finished text. Nothing is published without your explicit approval.

  4. An independent online magazine features you and what you offer

    After you approve the text, our editorial partner publishes an article in its own online magazine about you, your business, your services or your products.

    You then receive a direct link to the published feature.

What are you paying for?

The initial enquiry to the editorial partner is free.

Our editorial partners also charge no placement fee for publication under this arrangement.

You pay only for the service provided by Octopus: the automated creation of your article and the work involved in arranging publication.

Your introductory price: €25 instead of €50

Included in the price:

  • Article of up to 500 words
  • Free enquiry to a suitable independent editorial partner
  • Automated preparation of the article
  • Your approval before publication
  • Arrangement of publication
  • Direct link to the published article

No mandatory monthly payments. No subscription. No additional placement fee.

You decide whether one article is enough for now or whether you want more publications as your GEO presence grows.
