How Octopus works – one step at a time

Octopus isn't meant to overwhelm you with a giant questionnaire. It learns about your work in small, manageable steps, then turns verified knowledge into a strategy and content plan.

GEO Pass: the introduction

02 / 06Client need → Solution
STEP 02

Focus on your most important offer

We deliberately begin with one main priority: the service or product you want to make visible and grow commercially first. We explore who needs it, which problem it solves, how you work and what real value clients can expect.

Problem → Approach → Value

03 / 06Facts → Knowledge
STEP 03

Give your knowledge a clear structure

Octopus builds a knowledge base from confirmed facts about your experience, services and strengths. Your selected, commercially important offer becomes the lead offer and gets clear priority. Your other activities remain part of the full picture, but are organised in a meaningful hierarchy instead of being treated as equally important.

One fact + another + another

04 / 06Questions → Strategy
STEP 04

Create a personal GEO strategy

Which customer questions matter? What facts still need explaining? Octopus plans topics, formats and suitable channels around your business goals.

Question → Content → Channel

Start your free GEO Pass →
05 / 06Content → Channels
STEP 05

Make your expertise clear and visible

Octopus turns your knowledge into content that explains your services in the language of your clients. What problem do you solve? When should someone turn to you? What makes your offer different? It prepares suitable texts and materials for your website, LinkedIn, specialist articles and other channels. Before publication, statements are checked against confirmed facts. With your approval, content can be published through the agreed channels. Supporting technical information helps search and AI systems understand the context.

Website · LinkedIn · Industry media

06 / 06Review → Improve
STEP 06

Monitor, learn and improve

Planned reports will track how AI describes your work, when it mentions you and what information is missing. Each review helps shape the next improvement.

Monitor → Learn → Improve

Why do it step by step?

Small steps help prevent mixed-up services, contradictory claims and lost details. You shouldn't need to write a marketing plan or learn how crawlers work.
