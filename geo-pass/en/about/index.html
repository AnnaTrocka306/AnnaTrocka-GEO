Why GEO-Pass?

We are building an open professional register with transparent facts, direct contact and a structure designed for machine reading.

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization. Structured data helps automated systems distinguish professional roles, services and client needs. It cannot guarantee AI indexing or recommendations. Octopus onboarding and crawler statistics are planned, not live.

A project developed by Anna Trocka. Its future legal and organisational structure has not yet been determined.
