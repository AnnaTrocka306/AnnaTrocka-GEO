ABOUT THE PROJECT

GEO-Pass and Octopus — a project to build your business's AI presence

You run your business. We help make it easier for artificial intelligence to understand.

Imagine a potential customer describing a problem to ChatGPT and asking who could help.

Perhaps you offer exactly the right solution. But does AI understand what you do, which problems you solve and when someone should turn to you?

Knowing that your business exists is not the same as understanding why you might be the right person to help someone with a particular problem.

That is why we created this project.

01

Why is GEO becoming relevant for businesses?

Advertising, websites, search engines and social media remain important marketing channels. But potential customers are also turning directly to artificial intelligence to find suitable professionals and businesses.

When ChatGPT recommends a provider and explains why their services fit a particular request, part of the work traditionally done by marketing and sales can happen before the customer makes contact.

Yet AI systems do not always have enough reliable information about a business's specialisation or the specific problems it can solve.

This is where GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation comes in.

GEO focuses on making business information accessible and understandable to generative AI systems, so that professional expertise can be linked more accurately to people’s needs.

Our project brings together two complementary tools for this work: GEO-Pass and Octopus.

02

GEO-Pass — a free first step towards AI visibility

How can I make my business easier for ChatGPT to understand? Can I get started with GEO for free, even without my own website, technical expertise or a marketing agency?

We created GEO-Pass for people asking exactly these questions: a free, structured business and professional profile designed to be understandable to both people and AI systems.

It organises information about your specialisation, services and professional expertise. More importantly, it explains the specific situations in which you can help a customer.

For example, saying that you are a solicitor or lawyer is not enough. It also matters which legal problems people can come to you with.

This creates meaningful connections between a potential customer’s needs and the expertise and solutions a business can offer.

GEO-Pass is a free, standalone tool and an important first step towards building your business’s AI presence.

But our project does not end with creating a professional profile.

03

Octopus — automated GEO strategy, content and publishing

A single profile cannot always convey the full depth of your experience, specialist services or answers to the many questions customers may have.

To do that, businesses need additional reliable material that explains their expertise in different situations.

But how can you develop your GEO presence if you have no marketing department and no time to write articles and manage publications yourself?

That is why we are developing Octopus — an intelligent system for automating GEO processes.

The professional identity, specialisation and expertise recorded in GEO-Pass are intended to provide the starting context for Octopus’s subsequent work.

Using that context, Octopus is designed to identify relevant topics, develop a content strategy, prepare original material and organise its publication.

The project also envisages its own specialist editorial platforms: independent information publications offering genuinely useful, original content, rather than outlets for distributing identical advertising texts at scale.

Content must be based on real expertise, professional experience and trustworthy information.

Over time, this can help businesses explain what they do in a wider range of contexts and create additional information sources that AI systems may be able to discover and use.

You focus on running your business. Octopus is designed to take care of the content and publishing work that can be automated.

Octopus is still in development, and its features will be introduced in stages.

04

How can you tell whether AI bots access your GEO-Pass?

We are also developing analytics to help you understand when automated systems access a published GEO-Pass profile.

Once available, the feature is intended to show which identifiable AI bots requested your page and when those requests took place.

This can help assess whether the profile is technically accessible to automated systems.

However, we make an important distinction: an AI bot accessing a page does not mean that ChatGPT has recommended the business to a customer. Technical access and actual recommendations are different things.

05

What’s free, and which services will be paid?

GEO-Pass will remain free. Creating and publishing a basic professional profile will not require a subscription or the purchase of additional services.

Octopus is an optional next step. Its advanced content creation and publishing automation services will be offered on a paid basis.

You decide whether GEO-Pass alone meets your needs or whether you would like to develop your AI presence further.

06

Our GEO methodology: how does it differ from SEO?

The project and its independently developed GEO methodology were created by Anna Trocka.

Traditional SEO helps improve the discoverability and visibility of information in search engines.

Our GEO methodology also considers professional Identity, Role and Context. It examines meaningful links between specific customer problems, the circumstances surrounding them and the relevant skills and solutions a business offers.

It also considers cause and effect: what situation creates a particular need, and which professional capability could help address it?

Rather than merely listing services, we aim to explain who they are relevant to, when they are needed and why they may help.

GEO does not replace traditional marketing, nor does it guarantee recommendations from ChatGPT or additional sales.

Our aim is to make GEO accessible to business owners and automate as much of the ongoing work of developing an AI presence as possible.

You do not have to become a GEO expert. Focus on what you do best: running your business.
