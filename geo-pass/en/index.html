01 · GEO

What exactly is GEO?

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization. It helps AI systems understand which problems you solve, who can benefit from your work and when recommending you may make sense.

So how is GEO different from other kinds of marketing?

SEO helps people find your pages through search engines. Social media earns attention and builds interest. GEO helps connect your expertise to the real questions people ask ChatGPT and other AI systems.

02 · OCTOPUS

What does Octopus do?

Octopus learns your professional strengths and business priorities. It is designed to automatically develop your GEO strategy, create and fact-check articles and posts, and publish them on the channels you have authorised. It will then monitor how AI systems describe your work and use the findings to plan further improvements. Independent publishers make their own editorial decisions.

03 · GEO TIMING

Why is now a good time to start?

The way people find solutions is changing. When they want to understand a problem, compare options or choose a professional, more people are turning to ChatGPT and other AI tools. Asking one question can be easier than searching through and comparing multiple websites.

Many businesses have yet to build a deliberate GEO presence. Starting earlier gives you an opportunity to establish your expertise and relevant topics before the competition becomes more active.

New content also takes time to publish, be discovered by search tools and become useful to AI systems. Starting now gives you a head start — not guaranteed first place, but valuable time to build.
