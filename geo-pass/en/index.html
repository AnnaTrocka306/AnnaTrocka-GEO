01 · GEO

What exactly is GEO?

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization. It makes information about your work easier for AI to understand, including which problems you solve and who you can help. SEO helps search engines find your pages. Social media builds attention. GEO helps connect your expertise with questions and recommendations generated by AI – including Google's AI Mode.

02 · OCTOPUS

What does Octopus do?

Octopus is designed to learn your strengths and commercial priorities, build an individual GEO strategy, then prepare, check and distribute articles, posts and useful content across your chosen channels – from your own accounts to appropriate independent publications.

You stay in control of your goals and budget. No need to build a marketing department.
