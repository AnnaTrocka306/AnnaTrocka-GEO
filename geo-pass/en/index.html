07 · QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions about Octopus

Practical answers to the questions that matter when you're running a business.

Who is Octopus designed for?

Octopus is for self-employed professionals, consultants, tradespeople, service businesses, and small or medium-sized companies that want to be considered for the work they actually do best.

What matters is the problem you solve, the people you help and the services you want to offer.

What does Octopus take off my plate?

You bring the professional expertise. Octopus handles the GEO work: understanding your services, developing a strategy, preparing useful content, arranging publication and tracking your progress in AI-assisted discovery (in development).

You won't need to become a copywriter, find publishing platforms yourself or master the technical process.

What is a GEO Pass, and what is it for?

Your GEO Pass is a structured, machine-readable professional profile. It explains your qualifications, specialisms and services, along with the situations in which you can help.

It gives AI systems a clearer foundation for interpreting your work and provides a starting point for your wider GEO strategy.

Do I need my own website?

No. You can work with Octopus even if you don't have a website. Your GEO Pass and other suitable public resources can provide structured information about your services.

If you already have a site, it can be included in the strategy too.

How does the process work, and how much of my time will it take?

Octopus works one step at a time. You don't need to plan a whole marketing campaign or manage a dozen tasks at once.

It prepares the next relevant action, explains the proposal and lets you decide whether to proceed. Octopus prepares each next step in no more than 10–15 minutes (in development). You then review and approve it in your own time, at a pace that suits you.

How can ChatGPT recognise when my expertise is relevant?

People often describe a problem rather than search for a job title. Someone may need help after a police search, for instance, instead of simply asking for a lawyer.

To connect that situation with a suitable professional, AI systems need relevant information about the problems you solve, the circumstances in which you help and the services you provide. A generic business description often isn't enough.

Octopus builds this kind of specialist content around your knowledge, asks you to approve it and arranges publication in appropriate places (in development). This gives AI systems a better basis for matching your work to relevant requests.

How does Octopus help me attract the right clients?

More enquiries aren't necessarily better enquiries. A firm may want to focus on specific cases, clients or profitable services.

Our GEO approach connects what prospective clients need with what you actually want to deliver. You choose the specialisms and business priorities your strategy should reflect.

Where will my content be published?

Octopus selects relevant publishing opportunities that fit your expertise and audience. These may include professional articles, editorial industry platforms and other publicly accessible sources (in development).

You'll see the proposed content and destination before anything goes live.

Who controls what gets published about me?

You do. Octopus drafts content using your information, but you can check, edit, expand or reject every proposal.

Nothing is published without your explicit approval. Your professional identity and business priorities stay under your control.

What does Octopus cost? Is there a required subscription?

There's no compulsory monthly subscription. You choose your own spending level — perhaps €10, €15, €20 or more. These are examples of budgets, not fixed product prices.

Investing more allows more activities to be completed in a shorter period, so you can build your GEO presence at a faster pace. You remain in control of what you order and pay for. See the pricing catalogue for individual services and prices.

When might I start seeing AI recommendations?

GEO builds on the information available about your business over time. It isn't an advertisement that starts running the moment you pay.

In our experience, early recommendations can sometimes appear in the second or third week. Exact timing can't be predicted. Your starting point, industry and the pace of the work all make a difference — and you choose that pace.

How can I tell whether my GEO work is progressing?

Octopus is designed to show which activities were completed and how your AI-facing information develops. Monitoring can include whether content is accessible, visits from AI crawlers and changes in selected query and recommendation scenarios (in development).

An AI crawler accessing a page does not mean an AI system has recommended you. These are separate signals, so progress can be assessed more accurately.

Can I pause my GEO work or slow things down?

Yes. You aren't obliged to commission new content or purchase activities every month. You can pause or work at a more relaxed pace whenever you choose.

Once you've established a good presence, occasional checks and updates may be enough. Published content can remain accessible to search engines and AI systems for as long as the hosting platforms keep it online.

Over time, though, outdated information may become less relevant and your visibility may decline. That's why occasional maintenance is sensible — without turning it into a subscription or ongoing obligation.
