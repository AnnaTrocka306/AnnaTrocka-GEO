OCTOPUS · FOR PROFESSIONALS AND BUSINESSES

Focus on what you do best. Not on the daily struggle to win new clients.

Octopus will help you attract new clients through recommendations from ChatGPT and other AI systems — while you focus on your work.

01 · ONE PLATFORM

How can ChatGPT and other AI systems know what you offer?

ChatGPT and other AI systems draw on information available online to answer people’s questions. Before they can consider recommending you, they need to understand which services you provide and which problems you solve for your clients.

That’s exactly what Octopus takes care of.

Octopus automatically creates relevant specialist content about your services and expertise, then publishes it across suitable online platforms once you’ve approved it (in development).

This allows AI crawlers to discover and process information about you. It lays the groundwork for AI systems to recommend your services when potential clients ask for the kind of help you provide.

You’re the expert in your field. You shouldn’t have to learn digital marketing as well. Octopus takes care of that for you.
02 · PRODUCTS

What Octopus can do for you

Octopus becomes your personal virtual marketing assistant. It plans how to attract clients through ChatGPT and other AI systems, then puts the right marketing activities into action for you, step by step (in development).

Simple and straightforward: You set your goals, your budget and your pace. Octopus takes care of your GEO marketing (Generative Engine Optimization), so you don't need to learn digital marketing or deal with complicated technology yourself.
